# Specification

## Summary
**Goal:** Turn the Chocolate Day experience into a guided multi-step flow with more romantic messages and a dedicated virtual “box of chocolates” gifting step.

**Planned changes:**
- Convert the current single-page experience into a multi-step (at least 3 steps) flow with clear Next/Back navigation and a visible progress indicator.
- Keep the existing Dairy Milk Fruit & Nut reveal interaction accessible within the new flow (including the reveal modal).
- Add at least two additional steps with new English romantic/sweet message content (heartfelt tone; keep the favorite chocolate singular: Dairy Milk Fruit & Nut).
- Add a dedicated “virtual chocolates” gifting step featuring an open/closed box-of-chocolates visual and a lightweight, accessible “open/reveal” interaction that shows chocolates and a short romantic note.
- Add and reference new static image assets for the gifting step under `frontend/public/assets/generated` and ensure the new views work in light/dark mode.

**User-visible outcome:** Users can go through a multi-page Chocolate Day message flow with progress, read additional sweet romantic pages, still open the Dairy Milk Fruit & Nut reveal, and view a virtual gift box of chocolates they can “open” to reveal chocolates and a romantic note.
